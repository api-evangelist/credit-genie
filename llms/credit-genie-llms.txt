# Credit Genie

> Credit Genie is a consumer fintech app operated by Creditly Corp. (Wilmington, Delaware,
> founded 2019). It advances $10-$150 with no interest and no hard credit check against a
> linked bank account (Cash Boost), and layers spending tracking, cash-flow prediction and
> subscription detection on the same connection (Money Manager). A Line of Credit product
> and AskGenie, an AI financial assistant, are in early access.

Credit Genie publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/MCP surface, and no partner or integration program discoverable
from its public site as of 2026-08-11. Probes of api.creditgenie.com, developer.creditgenie.com
and docs.creditgenie.com do not resolve in DNS; /openapi.json, /swagger.json and /api-docs
return 404 on every host that does resolve. Everything below is a human-facing surface.

## Products
- [Cash Boost](https://www.creditgenie.com/cash-boost): Cash advances of $10-$150, no interest, no hard credit check.
- [Money Manager](https://www.creditgenie.com/money-manager): Spending tracking, budgeting and cash-flow prediction.
- [Line of Credit](https://www.creditgenie.com/line-of-credit): Early access.
- [AskGenie](https://www.creditgenie.com/ask-genie): AI financial assistant, early access.
